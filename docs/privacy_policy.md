# Privacy Policy for LensGuard

**Last Updated**: December 10, 2024

## Introduction

LensGuard ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application LensGuard (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Use the App's features
- Contact us for support

**Personal information we collect includes**:
- **Email address**: Used for account authentication and important notifications
- **Name**: (Optional) Used for personalization
- **Contact lens prescription data**:
  - Left eye diopter (e.g., -1.50)
  - Right eye diopter (e.g., -1.75)
  - Preferred lens brand and model
  - Current lens pair start date
- **Device tokens**: For push notifications (FCM tokens)

### Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information:
- **Usage data**: App interactions, features used, session duration
- **Device information**: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Analytics data**: App performance, crash reports, error logs

### Health Information

LensGuard collects health-related information, specifically your contact lens prescription. This is classified as sensitive health data under GDPR and is handled with extra care.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

1. **App Functionality**:
   - Authenticate your account
   - Store your lens prescription for wear tracking
   - Send reminder notifications
   - Match you with relevant price alerts
   - Sync your data across devices

2. **Improvement & Analytics**:
   - Analyze app usage patterns
   - Diagnose technical issues
   - Improve app features and performance
   - Monitor crash reports

3. **Communications**:
   - Send push notifications for:
     - Lens insertion/removal reminders
     - Lens replacement alerts
     - Price drop notifications
   - Respond to support requests
   - Send important service updates

We **DO NOT**:
- ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
- ❌ Use your data for advertising purposes
- ❌ Share your health data with retailers or lens manufacturers
- ❌ Track your location

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data based on:

- **Contract**: Processing necessary to provide the App services you requested
- **Consent**: For non-essential features like analytics (you can withdraw consent anytime)
- **Legitimate Interests**: For improving app performance and security

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at Rest**: Data stored in Firebase Firestore is encrypted at rest
- **Access Controls**: Firestore security rules ensure users can only access their own data
- **Authentication**: Secure Firebase Authentication with industry-standard protocols
- **Regular Security Audits**: We review and update security measures regularly

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide you with App services
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce our agreements

**Upon account deletion**:
- Your personal data will be deleted within **30 days**
- Some anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics
- Backup copies will be deleted within **90 days**

## Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

1. **Right to Access**: Request copies of your personal data
2. **Right to Rectification**: Request correction of inaccurate data
3. **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
4. **Right to Restrict Processing**: Request limitation of how we process your data
5. **Right to Data Portability**: Request transfer of your data to another service
6. **Right to Object**: Object to our processing of your data
7. **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for data processing (where consent was the legal basis)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: **privacy@lensguard.app**

We will respond to your request within **30 days**.

## Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

**In-App Deletion**:
1. Go to Profile/Settings
2. Tap "Account Settings"
3. Tap "Delete Account"
4. Confirm deletion

**Email Request**:
Send a request to **privacy@lensguard.app** with subject "Account Deletion Request"

Upon deletion:
- Your account will be immediately deactivated
- All personal data will be deleted within 30 days
- You will receive confirmation via email

## Children's Privacy

LensGuard is **NOT intended for users under 18 years of age**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 18, please contact us immediately at **privacy@lensguard.app**, and we will delete the information promptly.

## Cookies and Tracking

The LensGuard App does **not use cookies** (cookies are used only in web browsers). 

We use:
- **Firebase Analytics**: For usage statistics (anonymized)
- **Crashlytics**: For crash reporting
- **Device identifiers**: For authentication and notifications

You can opt out of analytics:
- Go to Settings > Privacy > Analytics
- Toggle "Share Analytics Data" off
